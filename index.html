🚀 Kerbal Space Program: Autonomous Ascent kOS

Automated gravity turn script executed via Kerboscript:

// Ascent Autopilot Script
CLEARSCREEN.
PRINT "Initiating launch sequence...".

LOCK THROTTLE TO 1.0.
LOCK STEERING TO HEADING(90, 90).
STAGE.

// Pitch down as speed builds
WAIT UNTIL SHIP:VELOCITY:SURFACE:MAG > 100.
LOCK STEERING TO HEADING(90, 45).

// Cut engines at desired apoapsis
WAIT UNTIL SHIP:APOAPSIS > 80000.
LOCK THROTTLE TO 0.0.
PRINT "Target apoapsis reached!".
